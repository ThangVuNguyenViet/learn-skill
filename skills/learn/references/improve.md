# Better ways

Recording shows how the person does the task today. Before saving, step back and ask whether the task itself has a better shape. The person decides; you propose.

## Look for

- **The goal behind the steps.** Name it in one sentence ("find which campaigns lost money yesterday"). Every proposal serves that goal, not the recorded clicks.
- **A standard practice.** Research how practitioners do this kind of task now: the method, the metric, the check a professional would add. Use the `research` skill (or web search) and keep 2–3 sources the person could open.
- **A ready-made skill or connector.** Search with `find-skills` (`npx skills find <task words>`) and the app's connectors. A candidate qualifies only when its source is reputable and it covers the step better than the recording does; give installs and source.
- **A step that can go.** Copying data between two sites the app can reach directly, checking something a rule can check, waiting for a page that has a direct link.

## Propose

Add a **Better ways** section to the before-saving retro, after the step table. At most three proposals, strongest first, each:

| | |
|---|---|
| What | One sentence in the person's words: "Compare each campaign with its own last-4-weeks average, not with yesterday." |
| Why | The gain, and the source or skill it comes from. |
| Cost | What changes for them: a new sign-in, an install, a different result than they're used to. |

Then ask which, if any, to use. Each proposal is its own yes or no. Nothing is installed, signed into or changed before a yes, and declining keeps the workflow exactly as recorded. A proposal they accept gets compiled and verified like any other step.

Offer nothing when nothing beats the recording; say so in one line.
